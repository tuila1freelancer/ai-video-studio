# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short: we want participation in this project to be a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or other project spaces.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews — and
when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through the contact listed on the
[@tuila1freelancer](https://github.com/tuila1freelancer) GitHub profile. Every report is reviewed
promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do
not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour they
judge harmful, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
